# 04 Selection

At this checkpoint:

- The ArcGIS vector basemap is displayed.
- Historic places appear on the map and in the sidebar.
- The map and sidebar represent the same dataset.
- Clicking a sidebar card selects its map feature.
- Clicking a map feature selects its sidebar card.
- The selected marker is visually highlighted.
- The selected sidebar card is visually highlighted.
- Only one place appears selected at a time.

If selection works in both directions, continue to filtering.
